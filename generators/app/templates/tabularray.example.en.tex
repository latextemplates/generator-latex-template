<%= heading2 %>{Tables with tabularray}

The \href{https://ctan.org/pkg/tabularray}{tabularray} package separates the layout of a table from its content.
The layout is given once as key-value list in front of the rows: the columns (\verb+colspec+), the style of whole rows (\verb+row{...}+), and cells spanning several rows or columns (\verb+cell{row}{column} = {r=..., c=...}+).
The rows then contain only the content, without \verb+\multicolumn+ or \verb+\multirow+.
\verb+X+ columns share the remaining width as in tabularx, \verb+Q+ columns are as wide as their content.
The caption is above the table, as for all tables.

<%- bexample %>
\begin{table}
  \caption{Runtime of four sorting algorithms, set with tabularray}
  \label{tab:tabularray}
  \centering
  \begin{tblr}{
      colspec = {X[l] Q[r] Q[r] Q[r]},
      row{1,2} = {font=\bfseries},
      cell{1}{1} = {r=2}{l,m},  % "Algorithm" spans the two header rows
      cell{1}{2} = {c=2}{c},    % "Runtime" spans two columns
      cell{1}{4} = {r=2}{r,m},
      row{4,6} = {bg=black!6},  % every second row on a light gray background
    }
    \toprule
    Algorithm & Runtime (s)  &                   & Memory (MB) \\
    \cmidrule{2-3}
              & 1\,000 items & 1\,000\,000 items &             \\
    \midrule
    Quicksort & 0.12         & 1.80              & 12          \\
    Mergesort & 0.15         & 2.10              & 24          \\
    Heapsort  & 0.20         & 2.40              & 12          \\
    Timsort   & 0.10         & 1.50              & 18          \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tblr}
\end{table}
<%- eexample %>

For a table spanning several pages, \verb+longtblr+ takes the same keys and repeats the header row (\verb+rowhead = 1+) on every page.
