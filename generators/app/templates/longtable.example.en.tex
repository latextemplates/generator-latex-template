<%= heading2 %>{Tables spanning multiple pages}

The \href{https://ctan.org/pkg/longtable}{longtable} package typesets tables that automatically break across page boundaries.
The header (\verb+\endhead+) and footer (\verb+\endfoot+) rows are repeated on every page; once enough rows are added, the table spans more than one page on its own.

<%- bexample %>
\begin{longtable}{lll}
  \caption{A sample long table.}\label{tab:long} \\
  \toprule
  \textbf{First column} & \textbf{Second column} & \textbf{Third column} \\
  \midrule
  \endfirsthead

  \multicolumn{3}{c}{{\bfseries \tablename\ \thetable{} -- continued from previous page}} \\
  \toprule
  \textbf{First column} & \textbf{Second column} & \textbf{Third column} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead

  \midrule
  \multicolumn{3}{r}{{Continued on next page}} \\
  \endfoot

  \bottomrule
  \endlastfoot

  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
  A & BC & D \\
\end{longtable}
